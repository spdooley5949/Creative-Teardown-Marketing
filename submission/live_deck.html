The opening

One brand owns belonging. Nobody else contests it.

Community is uncontested territory, and even its owner does not prove it.

What exists

Gymshark has made belonging half its message: coached couch-to-5K and half-marathon programmes. Alo has ALO Access. Everyone else: zero.

What is missing

Neither publishes a participation number, a completion rate or a member count. The claim exists; the proof does not.

The move

A named programme with published numbers, placed where competitors put discount percentages. Ten brands have ceded the lane to one competitor who is not defending it with evidence.

Spend against runners and gym training, not the everyday athleisure block where lululemon, Vuori and Fabletics are stacked.

What we got wrong

Our first answer was confident and false

At 42 ads we concluded three brands cited no proof at all. That was wrong.

BrandWhat we saidWhat they actually run
Vuori“The biggest proof gap in the group” 4.6 from 13,593 reviews, 100% product guarantee
Gymshark“Free returns only” 4.6 from 200 reviews
lululemon“Fabric adjectives only” Three separate ratings

The Transparency Center shows four ads until you click “See all ads.” Those four skew to product listings, which carry no ratings. We went back and collected the full lists. Then a teammate audited every tag and found 42 more errors, including in our own headline count — all corrected before this presentation.

What we learned

Five things the workbook does not show

  1. 01The bottleneck was collection, not intelligenceClassifying 101 ads took ninety seconds. Collecting them took hours.
  2. 02A shallow sample gives a confident wrong answerNot a vague one. That is far more dangerous.
  3. 03Model output drifts unless you freeze itTwo runs on identical data moved 14 tags and rewrote the headline.
  4. 04Most search advertising is not positioningOnly 16 of 101 ads were brand-level. The rest is product feed.
  5. 05The valuable output was the empty columnEvery recommendation came from what nobody said.

Everyone is selling the same fabric.
Nobody is selling belonging with proof.

That is the lane.
